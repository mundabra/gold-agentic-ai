# Pricing and discounts

## List prices

Single tracks cost $0.99; video tracks cost $1.99. An album costs the sum of its tracks. Prices are the same in every country; invoices are in US dollars.

## Discounts a rep can give

A rep may give up to 10% off any order of $20 or more without asking anyone. Record the discount and the reason in the account's activity log on the same day.

Discounts from 10% to 20% need a sales manager's approval before the quote is sent. Anything above 20% needs sales leadership. Never promise a discount you are not allowed to give; say you will come back within one business day.

## Bundles

Genre bundles (for example "Latin essentials" or "Classic rock") are priced at 15% below the sum of their albums. Bundles already include the discount, so no further discount applies to them.
