# Account management playbook

## At-risk accounts

An account is at risk when its last purchase was more than 180 days ago. Call every at-risk account within five business days of it appearing on your list. Lead with something new in the customer's favourite genres, not with a discount.

## Follow-ups

Every call, meeting or quote gets a follow-up date. Default: one week after a quote, two weeks after a first call, and next quarter after a happy check-in.

## Logging activities

Log each call, email and meeting on the day it happens, with one or two sentences: what the customer wants and what you promised. Never log personal data such as home addresses or phone numbers in notes.

## Quarterly reviews

Your top five accounts by lifetime value get a short review every quarter: what they bought, what changed, and one proposal.
