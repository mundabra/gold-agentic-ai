# Discount approval matrix (internal)

## Floor prices

The lowest price we accept on an Enterprise volume licence is $1,800 a month. Below that, the deal needs the finance director's sign-off.

## Approval levels

Sales managers may approve up to 20% off. Sales leadership may approve up to 35% off for twelve-month commitments. Nothing above 35% is approved without finance.

## Margin notes

Bundles run at a lower margin than single albums, which is why no extra discount is allowed on them.
