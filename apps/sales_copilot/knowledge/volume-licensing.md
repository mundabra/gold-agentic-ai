# Volume licensing for companies

## Who it is for

Companies that play music at events, in offices or in stores need a volume licence, not individual purchases. Typical buyers are event, facilities and internal communications teams.

## Tiers

- Starter: up to 500 plays a month, $49 a month.
- Business: up to 5,000 plays a month, $299 a month.
- Enterprise: unlimited plays, priced per quote, minimum 12 months.

## What to ask before quoting

Ask how many locations or events, the expected plays per month, which genres they need, and whether they need the licence for a fixed period (for example one quarter of events). A quarter-long licence is billed monthly at the tier price with no discount.

## Next steps

Send the quote within two business days of the call, and log the call with a follow-up date one week after the quote.
