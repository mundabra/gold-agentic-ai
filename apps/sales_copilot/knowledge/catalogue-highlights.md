# Catalogue highlights

## Latin

Our Latin catalogue is strong in Brazilian music: Chico Buarque, Os Paralamas do Sucesso and Chico Science & Nação Zumbi. It pairs well with jazz for customers who buy both.

## Rock

Rock is our best-selling genre. Classic artists such as Led Zeppelin, Deep Purple and Iron Maiden sell steadily; greatest-hits albums are the easiest first offer.

## Jazz and classical

Jazz and classical buyers tend to buy whole albums rather than single tracks, so album and bundle offers work better than track recommendations.
