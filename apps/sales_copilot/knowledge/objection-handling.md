# Handling objections

## "It's too expensive"

Show the cost per play, not the total: a Business licence works out at about six cents a play at full use. Offer a bundle before offering a discount, because bundles are already priced 15% lower.

## "We already use a streaming service"

Consumer streaming services don't license music for business use in public or at events. Our volume licence covers that, so it is a compliance question as much as a price question.

## "We only need one genre"

That is fine: a genre bundle is the right starting point. Point to the customer's favourite genres from their purchase history.

## "Send me something in writing"

Send a short email the same day with the quote, what is included, and the next step with a date. Log it as an email activity.
